Common questions

Why was information not detected?

Automatic detection suggests candidates. It cannot find every item, especially in difficult photos. Zoom in, review the whole document and manually cover any missed information before sharing.

Can I import PDFs or multiple photos?

Up to 500 PDF pages or 50 selected photos are supported, subject to file size and available memory. Encrypted PDFs need the correct password. Exported PDFs are image-only; source passwords, searchable text and digital signatures are not retained.

Is HideQuick completely free?

Version 1.0.2 and later include unlimited photo and PDF exports for everyone. No Pro purchase is required. If an older version shows an export limit, update when version 1.0.2 is available in your App Store region. Previous purchases remain in your Apple purchase history. Contact Apple about any previous payment or refund request.

Are my images uploaded?

Editing and detection run on-device. HideQuick has no image upload service. Cloud file retrieval can require network access. See our privacy policy for details.
