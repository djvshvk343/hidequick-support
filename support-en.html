Common questions

Why was information not detected?

Automatic detection suggests candidates. It cannot find every item, especially in difficult photos. Zoom in, review the whole document and manually cover any missed information before sharing.

Can I import PDFs or multiple photos?

Up to 500 PDF pages or 50 selected photos are supported, subject to file size and available memory. Encrypted PDFs need the correct password. Exported PDFs are image-only; source passwords, searchable text and digital signatures are not retained.

How do free exports and Pro work?

Three successful output generations are free. Saving or sharing the same generated output does not use another export. Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase for unlimited exports; see the price displayed by the App Store. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account if you already own Pro.

Are my images uploaded?

Editing and detection run on-device. HideQuick has no image upload service. Apple purchases, restoration and cloud file retrieval can require network access. See our privacy policy for details.
